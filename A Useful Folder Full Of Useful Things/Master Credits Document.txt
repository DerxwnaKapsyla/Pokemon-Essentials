This file serves as a complete repository of everybody who has assisted with the development of The Adventures of Ayaka in some way or another. There's no way I could have made it even remotely as far as I did without these people's help.

This list contains spoilers for stuff in The Adventures of Ayaka. I do not recommend reading this if you wish to remain unspoiled.

If you look at this list and you don't see yourself, and you know that you've assisted- PLEASE contact me. I want to make sure everyone is credited fairly, and I am extremely sorry if I left anybody out.

CR04: SPECIAL THANKS
----------------------------------------------------------------------
The individuals listed above assisted with contributing to the project, however there are certain people and communities I want to thank from the bottom of my heart for various reasons:
* Vendily:
    * You have provided so much help to me on getting various scripts working over the years, I don't think I could thank you enough for all of the help you've given me. From assisting with porting the PWT to walking me through the construction of my Vs. Transition System, you have helped me through some of the roughest parts of the development of this project.
    
* Lucidious89:
    * I am putting this here to thank you, but also to apologize for basically being the person who's been responsible for needing to update Essentials Deluxe as a result of their insane ideas. But, through it all, you assisted me with some of my crazier ideas, and helped me figure out how I could execute them better. The Medicine and Meimu battles could never have existed without your assistance and your hard work.
	
* Sorahn:
    * You saved my hide when you finished the commission for the title screen for The Last Adventure. Everywhere I looked before you, I just could not find anything or anyone. Several people offered to assist, several saying they'd do it for free, but they either ended up ghosting me or just being unable to follow through. When I saw your work I knew you were the one I wanted to pay to bring the vision I had in mind to life, and I was not disappointed in the slightest.
	
* FionaKaenbyou:
    * Initially I just wanted assistance with balance testing my game, but you went above and beyond in a different direction. You helped me revised the script for The Mansion of Mystery in its entirety so that it flowed much better structurally. I am eternally grateful for that.
    
* Love-Albatross:
    * There is so much I could say to you and about you- you have been more than willing to produce graphical assets for me on the drop of a dime, even when I didn't ask it of you. When I reached the end of development, and I realized all I had left was a bunch of graphics I needed, you messaged me and offered to take as much of it off my shoulders as you could- out of the goodness of your heart and because we're friends, you'd say. There are no words to describe my level of gratitude.
    
* Paradarx:
    * You allowed me to not only use your music, but you also contributed several tracks for me- seemingly out of nowhere, even. I am still grateful that you managed to make that Magician of the Twilight remix for me when I just couldn't find a decent remix anywhere online. It was a pleasure to meet you in person at TouhouFest. Keep on making incredible remixes.
    
* The Eevee Expo / Relic Castle Community:
    * You all have put up with me when I go on my late-night ramblethons, you all have lent me aid in the numerous help threads I have made, you all have been a positive reinforcement for my continued development of this project. The community as a whole is wonderful and a delight, and I look forward every day to seeing what everyone comes up with.
    
* The Dirty Cog Crew:
    * Every single one of you have basically been by my side for, god, I don't even know how long at this point. You have encouraged me, motivated me, inspired me- you believed in me, even when I didn't believe in myself. All the days and nights I spent agonizing over myself and this project, you guys were there to remind me that I made it- and that alone is something I should be proud of.
    
* And finally, Maruno:
    * I was there when you took over development of Essentials. I watched as you and all of the other contributors took this kit and made it into something special. A powerful, versatile tool, with a strong community behind it. I know I've almost assuredly gotten on your nerves more times than I can count, and I absolutely understand why. Whatever you do when you finish development on Essentials, I wish you luck on it and life. May you have the rest and relaxation you honestly deserve for all the years of working on this engine.
    
Thank you. All of you.

Never ever give up on your dreams,
- DerxwnaKapsyla
